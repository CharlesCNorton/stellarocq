# Stellarocq

Machine-checked plasma physics in [Rocq](https://rocq-prover.org). Force balance, flux-surface averages, Mercier stability and quasisymmetry of a [VMEC++](https://github.com/proximafusion/vmecpp) equilibrium are stated as theorems about the exact numbers in its wout file, and each is proven or decided by a checker extracted from a proof.

[Expr.v](theories/Expr.v) defines expressions with a real-number meaning and an interval evaluator proven to enclose it, over CoqInterval and Flocq, and [Physics.v](theories/Physics.v) writes in them the ideal-MHD residual `J x B - grad p` of the field reconstructed from a wout by VMEC's half-grid rule. [Force.v](theories/Force.v), [Series.v](theories/Series.v), [Recon.v](theories/Recon.v), [Continuum.v](theories/Continuum.v) and [Residuals.v](theories/Residuals.v) prove what those expressions compute: read at a free radius they are the components of `mu0 (J x B - grad p)` of the reconstructed field, with the curl written in the coordinates from the reconstruction's own derivatives (`continuum_force_coords`, `continuum_force_asym_coords`) and, through any Cartesian field that agrees with it along the coordinate lines, taken in Cartesian coordinates (`continuum_force`, `continuum_force_asym`), and read at a node they are VMEC's combination of the fields at the two half points (`node_residual`, `node_residual_asym`). [Checker.v](theories/Checker.v), [Cell.v](theories/Cell.v) and [Cover.v](theories/Cover.v) bound that residual at points, over cells covering a continuum of angles and over the volume between surfaces, and reversed they bound it away from zero. [Project.v](theories/Project.v) encloses the discrete Fourier harmonics of the residual at the modes a solver retains, which is the form of force balance a spectral solution converges. [Cover2.v](theories/Cover2.v) decides, column by column, that the cells of a verdict cover what they span. [Quad.v](theories/Quad.v) proves the midpoint rule over a cell, [Integrate.v](theories/Integrate.v) integrates over a lattice of cells it lays out itself, over the lattice or over exactly one period of each angle, reading its error bounds off its own enclosures, [Mercier.v](theories/Mercier.v) and [MercierRun.v](theories/MercierRun.v) assemble the Mercier criterion from such integrals computed in the same run, and the quasisymmetry residual is bounded the same way. [Identities.v](theories/Identities.v) proves what the reconstruction satisfies exactly, [Symmetry.v](theories/Symmetry.v) that a quasisymmetric field makes both certified quasisymmetry residuals vanish, [Energy.v](theories/Energy.v) that the Euler-Lagrange expressions of VMEC's energy are the ideal-MHD force, [Hypotheses.v](theories/Hypotheses.v) states the physical assumptions, [Kantorovich.v](theories/Kantorovich.v) carries the Newton-Kantorovich argument in the abstract, [Newton.v](theories/Newton.v) turns an interval Newton test on any system of expressions into the existence of a unique zero in a box, [Colloc.v](theories/Colloc.v) collocates the residual of Physics.v over a band of surfaces as such a system, a discrete equilibrium of the reconstruction, and bounds how far apart two states of its box are by how far apart their residuals are. [Wide.v](theories/Wide.v) is the interval evaluator over floats of any precision, through which Newton.v reads a system's outputs at the centre of its box when the certificate names a precision above binary64's. [HalfGrid.v](theories/HalfGrid.v) proves the node rule second-order consistent with the free-radius residual and carries consistency and stability to the convergence `Hypotheses.discretization_is_consistent` asks for. [VmecKernel.v](theories/VmecKernel.v) transcribes VMEC++'s half-grid Jacobian and metric kernels, which form their products from averages of node products where the reconstruction multiplies interpolated series, and proves the differences exactly: each is a sum of products of two differences between the nodes, so the two agree at second order in the radial step (`vmec_jacobian_difference`, `vmec_metric_difference`, `vmec_jacobian_second_order`). [Box.v](theories/Box.v) evaluates over a box of states, so that a verdict holds for every field whose coefficients lie in it; [Integral.v](theories/Integral.v) and [BoxCell.v](theories/BoxCell.v) integrate and bound a component over cells at every state of a box; [TrigPoly.v](theories/TrigPoly.v), [TrigExpr.v](theories/TrigExpr.v) and [Harmonic.v](theories/Harmonic.v) integrate exactly the components that are trigonometric polynomials in the angles and enclose the discrete harmonics of the rest; [Newcomb.v](theories/Newcomb.v) turns certificates of iota, the pressure gradient and a resonant harmonic of the Jacobian into a broken Newcomb condition; [QSFloor.v](theories/QSFloor.v) turns two harmonic ratios into a floor on the quasisymmetry defect over a box; and [Coil.v](theories/Coil.v) bounds the angle between a coil field and a trigonometric torus, over the whole torus or at the points of a grid, and carries the bound to an invariant torus nearby under the KAM step `Hypotheses.kam_nearby` states. [FieldKAM.v](theories/FieldKAM.v) proves the a posteriori KAM theorem for the field-line flow of a symmetric coil field (`field_kam_fourier`): a first torus whose error, frame, twist and field data satisfy the conditions of the iteration of [KAMIter.v](theories/KAMIter.v) has within 2 kP eps0 of it an invariant torus given by Fourier series on a strip, which carries the field lines at the torus's Diophantine rotation ([TorusLine.v](theories/TorusLine.v)), and [KFinal.v](theories/KFinal.v) proves that six verdicts of a checker extracted from the same development, true on their data, give those conditions (`cert_ok_torus`). [KLohner.v](theories/KLohner.v) follows every field line through a box of a plane with affine enclosures over binary64 intervals ([FloatRI.v](theories/FloatRI.v)), each step Taylor's formula at second order with the coil field's derivatives along paths ([FieldPath.v](theories/FieldPath.v), [FieldVel.v](theories/FieldVel.v), [KJet12.v](theories/KJet12.v)) and an a priori box ([FieldStep.v](theories/FieldStep.v)), and proves that when every box of a segment of the outboard midplane is carried out of a region, `R <= R_D2` where `cos(P phi) <= 0` and `R <= R_D` elsewhere, no invariant torus lying in the region meets the segment (`lescape_no_torus`). [KBracket.v](theories/KBracket.v) joins that verdict to `cert_ok_torus` on the same coil sources (`lcfs_bracket`), which puts the outermost invariant torus of the region, where it crosses that half-line, between the certified torus and the start of the segment. [FORMAT.md](FORMAT.md) is the certificate grammar.

## Build

```sh
opam switch create stellarocq ocaml-base-compiler.4.14.2
opam repo add coq-released https://coq.inria.fr/opam/released
opam repo add rocq-released https://rocq-prover.org/opam/released
opam install rocq-prover.9.0.0 rocq-core.9.1.1 coq-stdlib.9.2.0 \
             coq-mathcomp-ssreflect.2.4.0 coq-coquelicot.3.4.5 \
             coq-interval.4.11.4 coq-flocq.4.2.2 dune.3.23.1
make versions   # the installed toolchain against the pinned versions
make all        # proofs, extraction, checker binary
make kam        # the checker of the KAM certificate KFinal.cert_ok
make esc        # the checker of the escape certificate KLohner.check_lescape
make audit      # Print Assumptions of every theorem
```

[paper/stellarocq.tex](paper/stellarocq.tex) describes the equilibrium certificates and their results on VMEC++ output; `tectonic paper/stellarocq.tex` builds it.

`make static` links the same checker statically, and the [releases](https://github.com/CharlesCNorton/stellarocq/releases) carry that build for x86_64 Linux.

## Use

```sh
python gen/make_cert.py wout.nc cells.txt --cells --nodes 6 --nu 8192    # write a cell certificate
./extract/_build/default/main.exe --tighten --verify cells.txt cert.txt  # write its bounds and check them
python gen/verify_cert.py wout.nc cert.txt                               # compare the certificate with the wout
python test/run_tests.py --data DIR                                      # regression suite
```

Each script in `gen/` gives in its docstring the commands that produce its result, with the options of the paper's results among them. The checker also takes `--project` with `--spectrum` and `--band`, `--lower`, `--integrate`, `--taylor`, `--slot3`, `--mercier-run` and `--mercier`, `--newton` with `--newton-eval`, `--newton-centre` and `--stability`, `--int`, `--harm` and `--dharm`, `--bp` and `--bt` for points and coverings of a boundary, and `--qs`, and `gen/` holds the drivers for the Mercier profile, the Boozer stream function, the convergence study, the radial scan, the resonance study, the quasisymmetry ratio and floor, the solver invariances, the manufactured-solution errors, the free-boundary jump, Newcomb's condition and the comparison with SPEC. The Python tools need `numpy` and `netCDF4`, and `h5py` for a VMEC++ HDF5 output.
